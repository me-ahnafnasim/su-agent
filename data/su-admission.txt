Sonargaon University (SU) — Admission Rules, Documents and Process

Sonargaon University admission sessions (3 per year): Spring (January to April), Summer (May to August) and Fall (September to December). There is no fixed admission deadline; students can admit in any session.

Sonargaon University admission process: there is no admission test and no viva. Students can apply online through the SU ERP (http://sue.su.edu.bd:5081/sonargaon_erp) or the admission portal (https://su-digital.com/admission/), or admit directly at the campus. The Green Road campus is the main admission office. Admission hotline: 16701, email admission@su.edu.bd.

Sonargaon University admission documents: (1) photocopy of National ID (NID) or Birth Certificate; (2) photocopy of SSC and HSC/Diploma marksheets or certificates (for Master's, also the bachelor's marksheet/certificate); (3) one passport-size photograph.

Sonargaon University undergraduate eligibility: minimum GPA 2.5 (or second division) in both SSC and HSC or equivalent. O-Level in four subjects plus A-Level in two subjects with minimum GPA 2.50 (A=5, B=4, C=3, D=2, E=1). GED with an average of 450 marks in five subjects is accepted. American High School Diploma and IB are accepted. A combined SAT score of 1100 can replace the admission test. Dakhil and Alim (Madrasa board) graduates are eligible; Qawmi madrasa certificates are not directly eligible. A study gap is allowed and there is no upper age limit.

Sonargaon University GPA below 2.5: only Fashion Design & Technology (FDT) accepts a minimum GPA of 2.0 in SSC and HSC individually. Children of freedom fighters need a combined SSC+HSC GPA of 5.00 with an authenticated freedom-fighter certificate.

Sonargaon University engineering admission rules: engineering and science programs (CSE, EEE, ME, Civil, Textile, NAME, Architecture) accept only HSC Science-stream students; Arts, Humanities and Commerce HSC students cannot join them. Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in HSC/A-Level are required. For the Morning (SUN) batch of Science & Engineering programs the minimum is SSC GPA 3.00 and HSC/Diploma GPA 2.50; for the Evening (MOON) and Friday (STAR) batches the general rule applies (GPA 2.5 in each). If a student got 'F' in Physics, Chemistry or Mathematics and it was their 4th/additional subject, admission may be allowed with the Department Head's permission for engineering programs except CSE (the exception does not apply to CSE).

Sonargaon University Diploma-in-Engineering admission: a 3- or 4-year Diploma in Engineering from the Bangladesh Technical Education Board (BTEB), or a BTEB-recognized engineering diploma, with CGPA 2.5 out of 4.00, and SSC minimum 2.5. Alternatively a combined GPA of 5.0 (minimum 2.5 each) or a total of 6.00 with a minimum of 2.00 in either. Diploma students whose 8th-semester result is still pending cannot admit yet but can book a seat; students who passed the 8th semester can admit with the admit card or course-completion certificate and the published result.

Sonargaon University diploma semester waiver (Friday and Evening batches only): Civil, EEE, Mechanical and CSE diploma holders from matching technologies get a one-semester waiver; Textile diploma holders get a two-semester waiver. Example matching technologies — CSE: Computer Science, Electronics, Data Telecommunication & Networking, Graphic Design & Printing; EEE: Electrical, Electronics, Telecommunication, Electro-Medical, Instrumentation & Process Control; Civil: Civil, Surveying, Architecture, Environmental, Civil Wood, Construction. In the Morning (SUN) batch, including Architecture, there is no semester waiver: Diploma entrants study and pay the same as HSC entrants.

Sonargaon University Master's admission: a relevant bachelor's degree from a recognized university with GPA 2.5 or above on a 4.0 scale, or second division/class in all previous exams. Some students may need preparatory courses; some departments have extra requirements. SU's own graduates pay an admission fee of BDT 9,500 (including 1,000 tuition) and half the semester fees.

Sonargaon University ACCA and CA students (BBA): credit exemption is decided before admission by an Equivalence Report from the Central Equivalence Committee and cannot change after admission. Maximum 12 credits per semester. Credit transfer fee BDT 20,000, one time, payable by the 2nd semester. ACCA/CA students pay the regular BBA admission fee (BDT 12,500) and get no admission-fair or special discounts.

Sonargaon University re-admission: available to students who dropped out for 1 year (3 consecutive tri-semesters or 2 bi-semesters). Re-admission with the same ID costs BDT 6,000 (admission fee of the current offer). Starting fresh with zero credit and a new ID costs BDT 5,500 plus the current course fee. Keeping previous credits: only the current admission fee applies, and the higher of the current or previous course fee is charged.

Sonargaon University credit transfer from another university: national and international credit transfer is available, up to 50% of the required credits; courses must be at least 80% similar with a minimum grade of B, and the applicant needs CGPA 2.50 or more. Documents: application to the Registrar, SSC and HSC transcripts, official transcripts from all universities attended (including failed courses) and the syllabus of each course. Central Equivalence Committee approval is required. Credit transfer fee: BDT 20,000 (one time).
