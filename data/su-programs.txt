Sonargaon University (SU) — Faculties, Programs and Subjects

Sonargaon University has three faculties: the Faculty of Science & Engineering, the Faculty of Business (Business Administration), and the Faculty of Arts & Humanities. SU offers 13 undergraduate (bachelor's) programs and 9 Master's (postgraduate) programs.

Sonargaon University undergraduate programs for Science-background students (subjects that need Science in HSC): B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE); B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering (EEE); B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering (ME); B.Sc. in Civil Engineering; B.Sc. in Textile Engineering; B.Sc. in Naval Architecture & Marine Engineering (NAME); Bachelor of Architecture (BARC). Engineering admission requires Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in HSC or A-Level.

Sonargaon University undergraduate programs open to students from any background (Science, Commerce/Business or Arts/Humanities): B.Sc. in Apparel Manufacture & Technology (AMT); B.Sc. in Fashion Design & Technology (FDT); Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA); LLB (Honours) in Law; B.A. (Honours) in Bangla; BSS (Honours) in Journalism and Media Studies (JMS).

Sonargaon University undergraduate credits and duration (HSC entrants, all 4 years): CSE 162 credits; EEE 161 credits; Mechanical (ME) 160 credits; Civil 161 credits; Textile 161 credits; BBA 141 credits; AMT 160 credits; FDT 160 credits; NAME 161 credits; Architecture (BARC) 196 credits; Bangla 142 credits; LLB 144 credits; JMS 146 credits.

Sonargaon University Diploma-entrant credits: in the Friday (STAR) and Evening (MOON) batches, Diploma-in-Engineering holders study a reduced load — CSE, EEE and ME 144 credits, Civil 147.5 credits, Textile 140 credits — finishing in about 3 years 8 months (CSE: 11 semesters). In the Morning (SUN) batch, Diploma entrants study the same credits and semesters as HSC entrants (for example CSE: 162 credits, 12 semesters, 4 years).

Sonargaon University batch availability by program (Spring 2027): CSE, EEE, Mechanical and Civil run in all three batches — Morning (SUN), Friday (STAR) and Evening (MOON). Textile and BBA run in Morning and Friday. AMT and FDT run in Morning and Friday. NAME, Architecture (BARC), Bangla, LLB and JMS run in the Morning batch only.

Sonargaon University Master's (postgraduate) programs — 9 programs: MBA (Master of Business Administration, 39 credits, 1 year); EMBA (Executive MBA, 54 credits, about 1.4 years, for working professionals); RMBA (Regular MBA, 69 credits, 2 years, for fresh graduates); MSCM (MBA in Supply Chain Management, 69 credits, 2 years); MBM (Master in Bank Management, 69 credits, 2 years, with a mandatory thesis); MTFM (MBA in Textile & Fashion Marketing, 69 credits, 2 years); MAM (MBA in Apparel Merchandising, 60 credits, 2 years); MAMS (MBA in Apparel Merchandising, 36 credits, 1 year); LLM (Master of Laws, 36 credits, 1 year). A 2-year LLM is not offered.

Sonargaon University MBA majors: students in MBA, RMBA and EMBA choose one major — Management, Human Resource Management, Accounting & Information Systems, Finance, Marketing, Management Information Systems, or Supply Chain Management.

Sonargaon University BBA course examples (from the ACCA equivalence list): Introduction to Business, Business Communication, English I and II, Cost Accounting, Management Accounting, Financial Accounting I and II, Mathematics for Business Decisions I, Business Law, Income Tax and Practices, Business Finance, Micro Economics, Auditing Practices, Financial Management, Investment Analysis & Portfolio Management, Entrepreneurship Development, Banking & Insurance, Project Management, International Business Management, Strategic Management and International Financial Management.

Sonargaon University does NOT offer: a university hostel, fully online programs, MSc programs, Pharmacy, an English (subject) program, short courses, or a 2-year LLM. Semester-by-semester course lists for each program are not included in this knowledge base; students should check the department or https://su.edu.bd.

Sonargaon University complete program list (SU-তে যেসব বিভাগ/প্রোগ্রাম আছে): the 13 undergraduate programs (CSE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, Textile, NAME, Architecture, AMT, FDT, BBA, LLB, Bangla, JMS) and the 9 Master's programs (MBA, EMBA, RMBA, MSCM, MBM, MTFM, MAM, MAMS, LLM) are the COMPLETE list of programs offered. Any department or program not in this list does not exist at Sonargaon University. For example, SU has no separate department or degree in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning, Data Science, Software Engineering, Cyber Security, Information Technology (IT), Robotics, Pharmacy, Medicine, Nursing, Public Health, Microbiology, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Economics, English, Islamic Studies, Sociology, Political Science, Agriculture, Hotel Management or Tourism, and no MSc programs.

Sonargaon University closest related programs (for subjects SU does not offer as a separate department): AI, Machine Learning, Data Science, Software Engineering, Cyber Security, IT, programming or computer networking → B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE). Robotics, electronics, telecommunication, power or renewable energy → B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering (EEE), or Mechanical Engineering for mechatronics and automobiles. Construction, building or structural design → Civil Engineering; interior or building design → Architecture (BARC). Ship building, marine or naval engineering → NAME. Garments, apparel or merchandising → AMT, Textile Engineering or the MBA in Apparel Merchandising (MAM/MAMS); fashion → FDT. Economics, finance, banking, accounting, marketing, HR, management or business → BBA (and MBA, MBM or MSCM after graduation). English, literature or Bangla language → B.A. in Bangla (no English program). Media, journalism, communication or film → JMS. Law, judiciary or legal practice → LLB (and LLM). There is no related program at SU for medicine, pharmacy, nursing, agriculture or pure sciences.
