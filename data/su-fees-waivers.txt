Sonargaon University current fees are from the Spring 2027 fee sheet, effective from 14 October 2026. Total cost = admission fee + total semester fees + (credits x per-credit tuition fee). Result-based tuition waivers depend on the program, the batch (Morning SUN, Friday STAR, Evening MOON) and the student's combined SSC+HSC GPA band (for Diploma students, SSC+Diploma band in brackets). All amounts are in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT). For final confirmation call the admission hotline 16701.

Sonargaon University waivers and quotas (undergraduate): Golden A+ in both SSC and HSC/Diploma gets a 100% tuition waiver (not for LLB or Master's). Freedom-fighter quota: 100%. Female student: 10%. Sibling (brother-sister, husband-wife, parent-child): 10%. Disability: 10%. Tribal: 10%. Instructor quota: 10%. Group admission: 3% (2-4 students together) to 5% (5 or more). Admission Fair special waiver: BDT 30,000-40,000 on tuition (BDT 10,000 for LLB and Master's). SU sister-concern polytechnic students (NIET, Wii, NPI, BIST) pay BDT 9,500 at admission instead of 12,500.

Sonargaon University early-payment waiver: paying the full 1st-semester tuition together with the admission fee gives a 10% waiver on 1st-semester tuition; paying the full program tuition at admission gives a 15% waiver on tuition. This applies to both undergraduate and Master's students.

Sonargaon University Master's waivers: every Master's student gets the program waiver already built into the per-credit fee (no GPA bands). SU graduates also get the SU-graduate benefit (admission fee 9,500 and half semester fees), admission-fair students get BDT 10,000 off tuition, and these benefits stack. Personal quotas (female, sibling and so on), Golden A+ and freedom-fighter waivers do not apply to Master's.

Sonargaon University payment rules: tuition and semester fees are paid monthly, by the 10th of each month. After the 10th a late fee of BDT 50 per day applies, up to a maximum of BDT 500. A PVC fee of BDT 7,500 is paid in the last semester. Re-admission fee: BDT 6,000 (same ID). Credit transfer fee: BDT 20,000.
