Sonargaon University (SU) — Campuses and Facilities

Sonargaon University has three campuses in Dhaka: the Permanent Campus in Dasherkandi, Khilgaon (main campus), City Campus 1 on Green Road, and City Campus 2 in Mohakhali.

Sonargaon University Permanent Campus (main campus, স্থায়ী ক্যাম্পাস): Ward No. 75, Dhaka South City Corporation, Dasherkandi (Dasher Kandi), Khilgaon, Dhaka-1219, beside the Water Treatment Plant, near the Aftabnagar and Meradia areas. Academic activities at the permanent campus began in 2024. The campus is about 2 acres, with modern academic buildings, well-equipped labs, an auditorium, an open sports ground and green surroundings.

Sonargaon University City Campus 1 — Green Road Bhaban: 147/I Green Road, Panthapath, Tejgaon, Dhaka-1215, near the Kawran Bazar metro station. The Green Road campus is the main admission contact campus.

Sonargaon University City Campus 2 — Mohakhali Bhaban: GP Ja-146, Wireless Gate, Mohakhali, Dhaka-1212.

Sonargaon University facilities: 15+ modern laboratories, a library with 10,000+ books plus an e-library (https://lib.su.edu.bd), 24-hour campus WiFi, air-conditioned multimedia classrooms, on-campus medical service (Medimate Health Services), a prayer room, mental-health counseling, and the Center for Research, Training & Consultancy (CRTC).

Sonargaon University transport and hostel: SU provides free transport with a fleet of 20+ buses within Dhaka Metro City, for the Morning (SUN) batch only; there is no bus service for the Friday batch. SU has no university hostel; students are guided to nearby hostels and mess facilities.

Sonargaon University student life: 28 active clubs run by the Student Welfare Division (SWD, open 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily), including the Computer Programming Club, Robotics Club, Micro-controller Club, Mecha Club, Research & Development Club, Cultural Club, Theater & Film Club, Photography Club, Art Club, Design Club and Fashion Design & Career Club.
