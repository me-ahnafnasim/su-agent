Sonargaon University (SU) — Academic Rules and Regulations

Sonargaon University course registration: students register for at most 4 theory courses plus sessional/project courses per semester, generally 12 to 18 credits (the admission office currently advises up to 12 credits per semester in practice; confirm with the department). A course can be added within 7 days of the start of classes and dropped within 10 days, using a Course Adjustment Form through the adviser and department head. SU uses a fixed credit system, not an open credit system. All programs are taught in English except Bangla.

Sonargaon University credit rules: a theory course of 1 hour per week counts as 1.0 credit; a lab/sessional of 3 hours per week counts as 1.5 credits (0.75 per 3/2 hours). Course codes have a department code and a 3-digit number, for example CSE 101 or CE 400; the first digit is the year, and an odd last digit means a theory course while an even last digit means a sessional/lab course. Project/thesis courses use code 400.

Sonargaon University attendance marks: students need at least 75% attendance in each course; below 75% the student becomes non-collegiate (reconsideration possible with a fine and the Vice-Chancellor's permission), and below 60% the student cannot sit the semester final exam. Attendance marks: 90% or more attendance earns full attendance marks, decreasing to 40% of the marks for 60-65% attendance. A semester attendance below 75% makes the student ineligible for scholarships or stipends the next year.

Sonargaon University marks distribution: theory courses — class participation and attendance 10%, assignments/spot tests 10%, class tests/quizzes 10%, mid-semester exam 30%, semester final exam 40%. Lab/design courses — participation and attendance 10%, lab quizzes/viva 20%, final quiz 20%, sessional reports and performance 50%. Project/thesis — presentation and viva 20%, supervisor 40%, external examiner 40%. Class tests last 20-30 minutes; for a 3-credit course the best three of four class tests count.

Sonargaon University GPA and CGPA calculation: GPA = sum of (course credits x grade point) divided by total credits of the courses passed in a semester (F grades are not counted). CGPA is the same calculation over all semesters so far. Both are rounded to 2 decimal places. Courses with grade D or higher count as earned credits; an F in a core course must be repeated.

Sonargaon University CGPA to percentage conversion (official formula): for CGPA between 3.75 and 4.00, percentage = 79 + 80 x (CGPA - 3.75); for CGPA between 2.00 and 3.75, percentage = 44 + 20 x (CGPA - 2.00).

Sonargaon University supplementary, retake and improvement exams: Supplementary exam — for F grades only, at most 3 theory courses up to 10 credits, held within 2 weeks of results; maximum grade A-. Retake — for students absent from both mid and final exams or who failed the supplementary; register within 3 consecutive semesters; maximum grade A-. Improvement — only for courses graded below B+, at most 7 courses during a 4-year program, each once; maximum grade A-. Special Backlog — for final-year students with 12 or fewer credits left; maximum grade A-. Self-Study — 1 or 2 courses in the graduating semester when not offered; maximum grade A+.

Sonargaon University probation and semester withdrawal: a student whose GPA or CGPA falls below 2.00 is placed on probation; two consecutive semesters below 2.00 lead to suspension. A student who cannot sit the semester final because of illness or accident can apply for semester withdrawal (Incomplete) within 5 days after the final exam, with a medical certificate; this can be used once during the study period. Missing the semester final without a valid reason results in an F.

Sonargaon University honours, Dean's List and gold medals: an Honours degree requires CGPA 3.75 or higher; the minimum CGPA to graduate is 2.00. Dean's List: average GPA 3.75 or higher over the three regular semesters of a year with no F, retake or improvement. Vice-Chancellor's Gold Medal: first position in the department with CGPA 3.80 or higher and no F/retake/improvement. Chancellor's Gold Medal: first position in the department with CGPA 3.90 or higher. At the 2nd convocation (28 December 2025) SU awarded 96 gold medals: 13 Chancellor's and 83 Vice-Chancellor's.

Sonargaon University result re-scrutiny and graduation: a student can apply for re-scrutiny within 5 days of result publication (theory courses only); it checks only for unmarked answers or addition errors, with no re-examination. Answer scripts are kept for 3 semesters after results. To graduate, apply to the Controller of Examinations through the adviser and department head after completing the required credits and CGPA. Some departments also require industrial or professional training.
