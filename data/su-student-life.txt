Sonargaon University (SU) — Student Life, Clubs, Sports and Career Support

Sonargaon University clubs (28 active clubs under the Student Welfare Division): technology and research — SU Computer Programming Club, SU Robotics Club, SU Micro-controller Club, SU Mecha Club (SUMEC), SU Research & Development Club. Arts and culture — SU Cultural Club, SU Theater & Film Club, SU Photography Club, SU Art Club, SU Design Club, SU Fashion Design & Career Club. Professional development — SU Business Club, SU Entrepreneurship Club, SU Debate Club, SU Law Club, SU Moot Court Society, SU Media and Journalism Club. Sports — SU Sports Club. Service — SU Social Services Club, SU Environment & Disaster Management Club.

Sonargaon University Student Welfare Division (SWD): open 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily, director Zulkarnine Sultan Alam. SWD coordinates clubs, national-day programs, freshers' reception, farewells, study tours, blood donation, financial aid for needy students, job fairs and career counseling, and handles ID cards, dropped cases and program changes. Portal: https://su.edu.bd/swd

Sonargaon University events: International Mother Language Day (21 February), Independence Day (26 March), Pohela Boishakh (14 April), Freshers' Reception (September-October), Victory Day (16 December), farewell programs for graduating batches, blood donation camps and study tours.

Sonargaon University sports: more than 200 students play at Under-19, national and international levels, and many alumni have represented Bangladesh in cricket and football. In 2025-26, 7 SU students were in the cricket High Performance (HP) squad; SU players won awards in the Dhaka Premier Division 2025-26, and SU were champions of the Zia Inter-University T-10 tournament among 32 universities. SU also runs the SU Champions League football tournament and annual inter-department tournaments. Sports Division director: Mehrab Hossain Jeshi.

Sonargaon University career support: career mentorship, a Career Counseling Center (one-to-one guidance, CV review, interview preparation), a Career Development Program, an annual on-campus job fair, and internships across departments, including at large companies such as Walton and PRAN-RFL. SU does not guarantee job placement. Career portal: https://su.edu.bd/welcome/career. SU has 21,000+ alumni working at home and abroad.

Sonargaon University research and digital systems: research portal https://su.edu.bd/research/home, SU Journal https://su.edu.bd/Su_journal, Center for Research, Training & Consultancy (CRTC), Institutional Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC). Students use an eLearning platform and the SU ERP for class materials, results and academic information. Faculty list: https://su.edu.bd/faculty_members/all_faculty_details

Sonargaon University — what is NOT offered: no university hostel (nearby hostels and mess are available), no fully online programs (Friday and Evening batches include some online classes, but all exams are on campus), no guaranteed job placement, no MSc, no Pharmacy, no English-subject program, no short or certificate courses, no 2-year LLM, no bus service for the Friday batch, and no open credit system. LLB graduates can sit for the Bar Council enrollment exam (SU has Bar Council approval) and there is no age limit for it.
