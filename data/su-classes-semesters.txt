Sonargaon University (SU) — Classes, Batches, Semesters and Grading

Sonargaon University academic year runs from January to December. SU uses two semester systems. Tri-semester (3 semesters per year, 4-month semesters): CSE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, Textile and BBA (undergraduate), and MBA, EMBA, RMBA, MSCM and MBM (Master's). Bi-semester (2 semesters per year, 6-month semesters): AMT, FDT, NAME, Architecture (BARC), Bangla, LLB and JMS (undergraduate), and MTFM, MAM, MAMS and LLM (Master's).

Sonargaon University class batches and class times (Spring 2027): SUN (Morning batch) — classes 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM on any 3 days from Saturday to Thursday. STAR (Friday batch) — classes on Friday 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, plus additional online classes on Saturday and Thursday evenings. MOON (Evening batch, undergraduate) — 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM, with online classes on Saturday and Sunday and on-campus classes on Monday. MOON (Evening, Master's) — online classes on Saturdays and Mondays. All exams in every batch are held physically on campus.

Sonargaon University course load: students take 12 to 18 credits per semester. A 4-year bachelor's degree is completed in 4 years. Teaching follows Outcome-Based Education (OBE).

Sonargaon University attendance rule: 75% class attendance is required in each course; a student below 60% attendance cannot sit the final exam.

Sonargaon University evaluation (marks distribution per course): Continuous Assessment 30% (class tests, assignments/quizzes, attendance and class performance), Mid-Semester Exam 30%, Semester Final Exam 40%.

Sonargaon University grading system: 80% and above A+ (4.00); 75% to below 80% A (3.75); 70% to below 75% A- (3.50); 65% to below 70% B+ (3.25); 60% to below 65% B (3.00); 55% to below 60% B- (2.75); 50% to below 55% C+ (2.50); 45% to below 50% C (2.25); 40% to below 45% D (2.00); below 40% F (0.00). The pass mark is 40% (D). Minimum CGPA to graduate is 2.00; honours require CGPA 3.75 or higher.

Sonargaon University undergraduate admission requirements: minimum GPA 2.5 (or second division) in both SSC and HSC or equivalent; GPA 2.0 in each for Fashion Design and Technology (FDT). O/A-Level, GED (average 450 in five subjects), IB and American High School Diploma are accepted; a SAT score of 1100 can replace the admission test. For the Morning (SUN) batch of Science & Engineering programs the minimum is SSC 3.00 and HSC/Diploma 2.50. Engineering requires Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics in HSC/A-Level.

Sonargaon University Master's admission requirements: a relevant bachelor's degree from a recognized university with GPA 2.5 or above (out of 4.0), or second division/class in all previous exams. SU's own graduates get a special admission-fee benefit and a 50% waiver on semester fees.

Sonargaon University fee basics: the undergraduate admission fee is BDT 12,500 for most programs (NAME BDT 8,500), and the Master's admission fee is BDT 12,500. Tuition and semester fees are paid monthly by the 10th of each month; after the 10th a late fee of BDT 50 per day applies, up to BDT 500. Result-based tuition waivers depend on the program, batch and SSC+HSC GPA (up to 100% for Golden A+ in SSC and HSC, excluding LLB and postgraduate). For an exact program fee, contact the admission office (hotline 16701).
