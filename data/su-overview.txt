Sonargaon University (SU) — Overview, Owners and Leadership

Sonargaon University (সোনারগাঁও বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়, short name SU) is a private university (বেসরকারি বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়) in Dhaka, Bangladesh. It was established in 2012 and its academic activities began on 18 November 2012. SU is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the Government of Bangladesh. Its UGC university ID is 97 and it is serial number 63 in the UGC list of approved private universities.

Sonargaon University owners and founders: SU was founded by Engineer Abdul Alim and Engineer Abdul Aziz. The university is owned and run by the Sonargaon University Trust (সোনারগাঁও ইউনিভার্সিটি ট্রাস্ট), a registered, non-political, non-profit voluntary organization whose members are mainly entrepreneurs from the education sector. Chairman of the Board of Trustees: Engineer Abdul Aziz. General Secretary of the Board of Trustees: Engineer (Md.) Abdul Alim. Advocate Umme Salma is a member of the Board of Trustees.

Who owns Sonargaon University? (সোনারগাঁও বিশ্ববিদ্যালয়ের মালিক কে?) Sonargaon University is owned by the Sonargaon University Trust, founded by its two owners and founders: Engineer Abdul Aziz (ইঞ্জিনিয়ার আব্দুল আজিজ), Chairman of the Board of Trustees, and Engineer Abdul Alim (ইঞ্জিনিয়ার আব্দুল আলিম), General Secretary of the Board of Trustees. As a private university in Bangladesh, SU is run by this non-profit trust; the Vice-Chancellor (Professor Shamim Ara Hassan) heads academic administration.

Sonargaon University leadership (principal officers): Vice-Chancellor (VC, উপাচার্য): Professor Shamim Ara Hassan. Pro-Vice-Chancellor (Acting): Professor Bulbul Ahamed. Treasurer: Professor Dr. Mohammad Ekramol Islam. Registrar: S. M. Nurul Huda. The Chancellor is the Hon'ble President of Bangladesh (ex officio).

Sonargaon University deans of faculties: Faculty of Business — Prof. Md. Al-Amin Molla. Faculty of Science & Engineering — Brig. Gen. (Retd) Prof. Habibur Rahman Kamal, ndc, psc. Faculty of Arts & Humanities — Dr. A. S. M. Tariq Iqbal. Director, Student Welfare Division: Zulkarnine Sultan Alam. Director, Sports Division: Mehrab Hossain Jeshi. Admission Director (Admission & Branding): MD. Omar Faruque Molla.

Sonargaon University vision: to become a leading university and remain a top center of excellence in higher education and research, attracting brilliant students, teachers and scholars from all over the world. Mission: to impart world-class education and research facilities so students reach their full intellectual, social and personal potential, and to make higher education accessible in Bangladesh at an affordable cost. Academic tagline: "Quality Education, National Needs, Global Standards". Motto: "We will rise up, We will shine".

Sonargaon University in numbers: 350+ faculty members, 13 undergraduate programs, 9 Master's programs, 28 active student clubs, 15+ modern labs, a library of 10,000+ books, 20+ buses, and 21,000+ alumni. The 1st convocation was held on 13 April 2022. The 2nd convocation was held on 28 December 2025 at the International Convention City Bashundhara (ICCB), Dhaka, where 96 gold medals were awarded.

Sonargaon University accreditation and awards: SU is linked with the UGC, the Ministry of Education, BAETE (Board of Accreditation for Engineering and Technical Education) and BANBEIS. Awards include the a2i National Award 2018 (Innovation Competition), Asia Young Designer Award 2019, A3F Sustainable Design Award 2019, Shah Cement DOT Student Design Award 2019, and ISIF 2025 winner and bronze medal (SU NextGen Innovators).
